# Etched Refabricated

Turn any audio link into a music disc you can play in Minecraft.

**This is an unofficial fork.** It is not made, endorsed or supported by Moonflower Studio, the team behind Etched. Please report problems with it on [our GitHub](https://github.com/FugginBeenus/etched-refabricated/issues), not to them.

Etched Refabricated is a Fabric continuation of Etched. Etch a link onto a blank disc at the Etching Table, give it a label and an album cover, and play it anywhere vanilla discs play, plus a few places they don't. This fork picks the mod back up on modern Fabric, fixes long-standing bugs, adds new features, and ships a full set of new art.

## Features

- **Etch your own discs.** Paste a link at the Etching Table and get a disc that plays it. Direct audio files, SoundCloud, Bandcamp and more.
- **Plays the formats you have.** OGG, WAV, FLAC, MP3 and AAC (`.m4a`).
- **Labels and album covers.** Give discs a custom label, and albums their own cover art, including your own images.
- **Speakers.** Place one beside a jukebox and the music plays from the speaker. Set each speaker's volume, and quieter speakers carry a shorter distance.
- **The Stereo.** Sit it on a jukebox to drive speakers wirelessly. Preamps let it drive more speakers, and transmitters let it reach further.
- **Boombox.** Carry your music with you. It rests on your shoulder while it plays.
- **Album Jukebox.** Load up to nine discs or albums and it plays them in order.
- **Radio.** Give it a link to a live stream, or tune it to a Radio Station. A redstone signal switches it off.
- **Radio Station.** Fill it with up to 18 discs and go on air. Every radio tuned in plays the same track at the same moment, even for a listener who tunes in late.
- **And more.** A Minecart with Jukebox, note block support, and a Bard villager who runs a music stall in villages.

Vanilla music discs play through speakers too, not only etched ones.

## Known gaps

- Discs from this mod don't work in Sophisticated Backpacks' Jukebox Upgrade. Its Fabric port predates the disc handler API that would make this possible. Once updated, the feature will be implemented.
